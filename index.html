Seaglass Launcher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25 · applies to version 3.0.0 and later

Seaglass Launcher ("the app") is a Home screen app for Android, published by darwvin (contact: darwvin11@gmail.com). This policy describes what the app accesses on your device and where that information goes.

Summary

  • The app has no internet permission. It cannot send anything to the developer or to any server, and it contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • The app has no accounts and the developer receives no data from it.
  • Everything the app stores stays in its private storage on your device and is excluded from Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

What the app stores on your device

Data Why Where it goes
Home layout, settings, saved layouts, renamed apps, favourites, hidden apps To show your Home screen the way you arranged it App-private storage only
How often and when you open apps from the drawer "Most used" and "Recent" sorting in the drawer App-private storage only; not included in backups
Pictures you choose as a wallpaper or app icon To display them Copied (downscaled) into app-private storage
Contacts you add to the Contact Cards widget (name, phone number, photo) To show the card and dial the number when you tap Call Stored in the widget's settings on the device
Notes and goals you type into the Slate Notes and Voyage Goals widgets To show them Stored in the widget's settings on the device

When you export a backup, the app writes one file containing your layout, settings, widget settings (including contact cards, notes and goals) and chosen pictures to the location you pick through Android's file picker. The app does not upload it anywhere; what happens to the file afterwards (for example, if you save it to a cloud folder) is up to you and that service.

What the app reads on your device, only when you use the related feature

Access Used for Stored?
List of installed apps and their icons Showing apps in the drawer and on Home Only app identifiers you place on Home or mark as favourite/hidden
Contact picker (no contacts permission) Android's picker shares only the person you choose with the Contact Cards widget Name, number and photo of that person, as above
Calendar (READ_CALENDAR, asked when you add the Chart Calendar widget) Marking this month's events on the widget No — read when the widget is shown
Physical activity (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION, asked when a Voyage Goals step goal needs it) Today's step count for the step goal Only the day's step baseline and count
Network and Bluetooth state Wi-Fi and Bluetooth indicators on the Ship's Status widget No
Notification access (optional, turned on by you in Android settings) Notification dots, and message / missed-call counts on the Message Bell widget. Only the app, notification category and number of messages are used; titles and message text are not read or stored No
"Seaglass gestures" accessibility service (optional, turned on by you after an in-app explanation) Locking the screen or opening Recent apps from a Home gesture you assigned. The service cannot read window content and collects nothing No
Set wallpaper "Use on lock screen", and putting the theme's painting behind Recent apps No
Uninstall requests "Uninstall" in an app's menu; Android always asks you to confirm No

Refusing or later revoking any of these keeps the rest of the launcher working; only the related widget or gesture stops working.

Web search

If you search the web from the drawer, the app opens your browser (or the search engine you chose: Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing or Brave) with your search text. That search is then handled by the browser and search engine under their own privacy policies; the app itself does not send or keep it.

Third-party icon packs

If you choose an installed icon pack, the app reads that pack's icons on your device. No data is sent to the pack's developer by Seaglass Launcher.

Children

The app is a general-audience Home screen and is not designed for children.
